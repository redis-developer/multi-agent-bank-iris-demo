# Balance Transfer Guide

product: balance_transfer

## What is a balance transfer

A balance transfer moves the outstanding principal of a loan you hold with
another lender (or an internal loan on an older rate) to a new loan with us at
a lower interest rate. The bank pays off your old lender directly and your EMI
restarts with the new rate and tenure.

## Eligibility and savings

Loans with at least 12 EMIs paid at the existing lender and no overdue amounts
qualify. The new rate is typically 1–3% lower than the transferred loan's
rate. Example: transferring ₹5,00,000 outstanding at 14% with 36 months left
to 11% saves roughly ₹24,000 in total interest. A balance transfer makes sense
when the rate saving exceeds the transfer costs and you have at least 12
months of tenure remaining.

## Charges and process

Processing fee is 1% of the transferred amount (often waived in campaigns).
Your existing lender may charge a foreclosure fee — check their schedule.
Process: share your latest loan statement and foreclosure letter, we sanction
the new loan, issue payment directly to the old lender, and collect the
original NOC from them. End-to-end takes 5–7 working days.

## Top-up with balance transfer

You can combine a balance transfer with a top-up: transfer the outstanding and
borrow extra in the same loan at the new lower rate. The combined amount is
subject to standard eligibility (income, credit score 700+, FOIR under 55%).
