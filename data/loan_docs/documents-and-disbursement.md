# Documents and Disbursement Process

product: general

## Documents required

Salaried applicants: PAN, Aadhaar (or passport/voter ID), last 3 months'
salary slips, and last 6 months' bank statement of the salary account.
Self-employed: PAN, Aadhaar, last 2 years' ITR with computation, 12 months'
current-account statement, and business proof (GST/udyam registration).
Pre-approved customers: only PAN + Aadhaar e-verification and e-sign — no
income documents. All documents can be uploaded on WhatsApp as photos or PDFs.

## Document qualification

Uploaded documents are auto-checked for: readability, name match with the
application, PAN–Aadhaar match, statement completeness (no missing months),
and salary credit consistency. If a document fails qualification the bot lists
exactly what to re-upload. Manual verification, when triggered, adds 1 working
day.

## LAN generation and sanction

Once documents qualify and the credit decision passes, a Loan Account Number
(LAN) is generated. The LAN is your loan's identity for the rest of its life —
quote it for any service request. The sanction letter (amount, rate, tenure,
EMI, charges) is sent on WhatsApp and must be accepted by e-sign within 15
days or the sanction lapses.

## Disbursement

After e-sign and, where applicable, auto-debit (eNACH) registration,
disbursement is initiated to your verified bank account. Pre-approved loans
disburse within 4 business hours; standard loans within 24–48 hours of
sanction. The first EMI is due on the 5th of the following month (a pro-rata
"broken period" interest for the gap is added to the first EMI). You receive
the disbursement advice and amortisation schedule on WhatsApp.
