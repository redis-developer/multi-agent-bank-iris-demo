# Home Decor Loan Guide

product: home_decor_loan

## What the loan covers

The home decor loan funds interior work on a home you own or rent: modular
kitchens, wardrobes, furniture, painting, false ceilings, lighting,
furnishings, and smart home fittings. Amounts range from ₹1,00,000 to
₹15,00,000 with tenures of 12 to 60 months. Disbursal can go to you or
directly to an empanelled interior brand for extra discounts.

## Eligibility and rates

Same base eligibility as a personal loan (21–58 years, ₹25,000+ net monthly
income, credit score 650+). Rates run 12.5%–15.5% per annum reducing balance.
Customers renovating a self-owned home get 0.5% off the card rate. Processing
fee is 1% of the sanctioned amount.

## Partner benefits

Booking through empanelled partners (interior design studios and furniture
brands) gets you 5–10% partner discounts, a 12-month workmanship warranty on
empanelled contractors, and no-cost EMI conversion on select furniture
categories. Ask for the current partner list on WhatsApp by typing
"decor partners".

## Documents and disbursal

Existing customers: just a quotation or estimate from the vendor/designer.
New customers: KYC, last 3 months' salary slips or 2 years' ITR, and the
quotation. Disbursal within 48 hours of document verification. For direct
vendor disbursal, the vendor invoice replaces the quotation at payout time.
