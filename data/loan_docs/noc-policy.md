# No Objection Certificate (NOC) Policy

product: noc

## What an NOC is and why it matters

A No Objection Certificate (NOC), also called a loan closure certificate,
states that the loan is fully repaid and the bank has no further claim against
you for that loan account (LAN). Keep it safely: it is required to update
credit bureau records, release hypothecation, and as proof in any future
dispute. Without an NOC your credit report may continue to show the loan as
active.

## Eligibility for NOC issuance

An NOC is issued only after the loan status is "closed": all EMIs and charges
are paid and the outstanding balance is zero. For foreclosed loans, the NOC is
issued after the foreclosure amount clears. NOCs cannot be issued for active
loans, loans with pending charges, or loans under settlement (settled loans
receive a settlement letter instead, which is different from an NOC).

## How to get your NOC

On WhatsApp, an existing customer can request an NOC by verifying their
registered mobile number and the LAN. The digital NOC (PDF, digitally signed)
is generated within 15 working days of closure and sent on WhatsApp and email
at no charge. A physical copy can be couriered on request for ₹250. Duplicate
NOCs for loans closed more than 3 years ago take up to 21 working days.

## Common NOC issues

If your loan shows closed but the NOC has not arrived after 15 working days,
raise a service request — the bot can do this for you. If the credit bureau
still shows the loan active 45 days after NOC issuance, share the NOC in a
bureau dispute; the bank refreshes bureau records in the next monthly cycle.
