# Personal Loan Product Guide

product: personal_loan

## Eligibility

Salaried applicants aged 21–58 with a minimum net monthly income of ₹25,000 and
at least 12 months of continuous employment are eligible. Self-employed
applicants need 2 years of business vintage and an ITR for the last 2 years.
A credit score of 700 or above qualifies for the best rates; scores between
650 and 699 are considered with a rate premium of up to 1.5%. Existing
customers with 12+ months of clean repayment history get relationship pricing
0.25% below the card rate.

## Interest rates and charges

Personal loan interest rates range from 10.99% to 16.50% per annum (reducing
balance), based on credit score, income, and relationship history. Processing
fee is 1.5% of the sanctioned amount (minimum ₹1,999), waived for pre-approved
offers. Late payment charge is 2% per month on the overdue EMI. Loan amounts
range from ₹50,000 to ₹25,00,000 with tenures of 12 to 60 months.

## Foreclosure and part-prepayment

Foreclosure is allowed after 6 EMIs with a foreclosure charge of 4% of the
outstanding principal (nil for floating-rate loans). Part-prepayment of up to
25% of the outstanding principal is allowed once per financial year at no
charge, after 12 EMIs. Foreclosure requests are processed within 7 working
days; the No Objection Certificate (NOC) is issued within 15 working days of
loan closure.

## Benefits and considerations

Benefits: no collateral, disbursal within 24 hours for pre-approved customers,
flexible tenure, no end-use restriction, and EMI holiday of up to 60 days on
select schemes. Considerations: rates are higher than secured loans, missed
EMIs affect your credit score, and foreclosure within the first 6 months is
not permitted. Compare the total interest outgo across tenures before
choosing — a longer tenure lowers the EMI but increases total interest paid.
