# Pre-approved Offers Policy

product: preapproval

## What pre-approved means

A pre-approved offer is a credit line the bank has already sanctioned for you
based on your repayment history, account behaviour, and bureau score — before
you ask. Accepting one skips income verification: you confirm KYC, e-sign the
agreement, and funds disburse within 24 hours (often within 4 business hours).
Pre-approved offers carry zero processing fee and a rate at or below the card
rate.

## Checking and accepting an offer on WhatsApp

Existing customers can type "my offers" on WhatsApp to see live pre-approved
offers with amount, rate, and validity. To accept, reply with the offer you
want; the bot confirms terms, triggers e-sign, generates the Loan Account
Number (LAN), and schedules disbursement. Offers are valid until the date
shown and amounts cannot be increased without a full application.

## Why offers change or disappear

Offers refresh monthly from the bank's risk models. An offer can shrink or be
withdrawn if a bureau score drops, an EMI bounces anywhere, or credit
utilisation rises sharply. Using more than 3 hard inquiries in 60 days also
suppresses offers. Keeping EMIs on auto-debit and utilisation under 30% keeps
offers healthy.

## Cross-sell eligibility

Customers with an active loan and 12+ clean EMIs typically see top-up offers.
Customers paying a higher rate elsewhere see balance-transfer offers. Salary
account customers with no active loan see fresh personal loan offers. Offers
are always optional — declining one never affects your existing relationship.
