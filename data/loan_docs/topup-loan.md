# Top-up Loan Guide

product: topup_loan

## What is a top-up loan

A top-up loan is an additional loan on top of your existing active loan with
the bank. Because the bank already has your KYC and repayment history, top-up
loans need minimal documentation and disburse faster than a fresh loan —
usually within 4 business hours for pre-approved customers.

## Eligibility for a top-up

You must have an active loan with at least 12 EMIs paid, no EMI bounces in the
last 6 months, and a current credit score of 700+. The top-up amount is capped
at 70% of the principal already repaid, or the pre-approved amount shown in
your offers, whichever is lower. The top-up tenure cannot exceed the remaining
tenure of the base loan plus 12 months.

## Rates, charges and repayment

Top-up loans price at the base loan's rate minus 0.25% for customers with a
clean repayment record. Processing fee is 0.5% (waived on pre-approved
offers). The top-up EMI is billed together with the base loan EMI on the same
due date as a single combined debit. Foreclosure terms mirror the base loan.

## Why take a top-up instead of a new loan

A top-up avoids fresh documentation, income proof, and a new hard credit
inquiry. It is usually 0.5–1% cheaper than a new personal loan and the
combined single EMI is easier to manage. Consider a new loan instead if you
need a tenure much longer than your base loan's remaining tenure.
